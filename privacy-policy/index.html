Privacy
Policy

Globber is built to protect your privacy. It runs entirely on your device.

  • No internet permission
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No ads or third-party SDKs
  • No accounts

01Data Globber accesses

DataWhyWhere it goes
Incoming call numbersMatched against your block rules through Android's CallScreeningServiceProcessed on-device. A block log is stored only on your device.
Allowed numbersNumbers you choose to always let ringStored only on your device.
NotificationsTell you when a call was blockedShown on your device only.

02Data storage

Block rules, allowed numbers and the block log are stored in a local database on your device. Uninstalling the app removes this data. Globber never sends it to any server. If you have Android's own device backup turned on, Android may include it in your personal Google account backup, which Globber cannot read.

03Permissions

Globber requests a single permission:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS to tell you when a call is blocked.

It also asks to be your call screening app, a system role rather than a permission, which lets Android show it the number of each incoming call. Globber does not request contacts, internet, location, microphone, camera or storage access.

04Children

Globber is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

05Changes

This policy may be updated alongside app releases. Material changes are noted in the changelog.
