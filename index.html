How it works

Three steps to a quiet phone.

01

Write one pattern

A rule for the whole series, not the number.

Spammers rotate through hundreds of numbers from the same block. Type the prefix they share, +91 140, and every one of them is covered.

02

Let it screen

Stopped before your phone even rings.

Globber sits in Android’s call-screening slot. Matching calls are rejected, silenced or sent to voicemail in milliseconds, entirely on-device.

Questions

Good to know.

Does Globber need my contacts?

No. Globber never reads your contacts. Android only routes calls from unknown numbers to call-screening apps, so people you know ring as usual.

Will it block calls from real people?

Only if a rule matches them. Add any number to Allowed numbers and it always rings, whatever your rules say.

What happens to blocked calls?

You choose per rule: reject, silence, or send to voicemail. Each one lands in the log with the pattern that caught it.

Does anything leave my phone?

Nothing. The app has no internet permission at all, so it physically cannot send data anywhere.

Which phones does it support?

Android 10 and newer. It uses the official CallScreeningService, so no root and no hacks.
